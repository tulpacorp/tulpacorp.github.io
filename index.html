About

TULPA is a design research and consultancy practice working across architecture, language, craft, and technology. We help clients translate cultural and technical complexity into strategy, identity, and form.

Our work moves fluidly between speculative research and applied practice — building frameworks that make new ideas legible, and giving emerging concepts the shape they need to enter the world.

We are based in California and New York. Reach us at info@tulpacorp.com.

Project

art-ifice

art-ifice is a transmedial research group and art practice formed within TULPA by Nima Shariat Zamanpour and Brian Christopher Orser. The collaboration weaves conceptual and technical connections between architecture, language, craft, and technology.

An emphasis on workflows and processes carries the work from one medium to the next, opening various doors into the domain of space as it is constructed historically and digitally — from classical orders to machine-learned form.
